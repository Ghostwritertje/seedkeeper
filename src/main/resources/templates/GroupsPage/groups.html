How verdicts are decided

What the next run would do, decided right now: it removes the groups in the Remove lane. Torrents that share files (cross-seeds) are one group, with one verdict. A torrent of a managed tracker that its tracker has deleted (unregistered) leaves its group and is removed on its own; any other tracker message is only shown. The reason is the first rule that holds; the secondary reasons are the others that hold too. The forecast is when a group kept for its minimum seed time is expected to have met it, if it keeps seeding. The size is the data on disk of the group's wanted files, a file its members share counted once. It is not the space a removal frees: a file with a library link stays on disk.

Each lane has its own order: Keep by soonest forecast, Left alone by reason, Remove largest first. Choose a sort to put every lane in that order instead. A lane shows its first 50 groups; "Show all" shows that lane alone, in pages.

Set a keep override to stop a group from being removed (it never causes a removal): it gets the info hash of every member, and the members the group gains later are added to it. It shows in the Overrides view, where it can be edited or deleted.

{! One lane per verdict, Keep, Left alone, Remove from left to right; small screens stack them in the same order. The heading says the verdict in words, the colour only repeats it. Each group is a card: its name (a long name may break after a dot or a dash, , not inside a word), what it is and its size, the reason with the secondary reasons, what to do about it, the tracker message, the progress toward the minimum seed time while below it, and the members that cause the reason. The members of a group of more than one open on demand. !}
