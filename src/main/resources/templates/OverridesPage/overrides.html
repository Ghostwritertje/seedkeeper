A keep override is a manual instruction never to remove a group, until an optional end date (inclusive). It only prevents a removal. An expired override is past its end date, a stale one has no torrent left; neither has any effect, and they stay until someone deletes them. They are kept in {file}, which can be edited by hand: an edit shows here without a restart.
