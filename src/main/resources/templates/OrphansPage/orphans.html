Dangling symlinks

{#if danglingSymlinks.groups.isEmpty}

No dangling symlink is an orphan.

{#else}

A symlink dangles when the file it leads to is gone: most often a cross-seed link whose original torrent was removed with its data. It takes no space. Once you are sure its target won't come back, delete the link itself by hand.
